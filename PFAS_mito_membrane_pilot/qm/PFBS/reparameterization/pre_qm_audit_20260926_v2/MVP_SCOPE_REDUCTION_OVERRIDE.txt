============================================================

NOT ask:

"How should the full PFBS force field be completed?"

"What is the minimum additional parameter correction and

- PFBS local membrane association / insertion behavior

- publication-grade absolute free energies
- highly accurate PMFs
- diffusion benchmarking
- transferable standalone PFBS force-field claims
- complete vibrational reproduction
- exhaustive conformational PES characterization

============================================================
MVP RED-FLAG PRIORITY
============================================================

Only deficiencies likely to materially distort the intended
membrane MD should drive further refinement.

Priority order:

1. major equilibrium-geometry defects;
2. wrong dominant low-energy T_CC basin;
3. major T_CS low-energy/topology artifact;
4. instability of GEO_A / GEO_B under the same parameter set;
5. obvious parameter-induced structural artifact in short
   water/membrane pilots.

Minor residual errors that do not materially alter these
features are NOT sufficient reasons for continued force-field
development.
