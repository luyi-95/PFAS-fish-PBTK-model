# Proposed release hierarchy

All stages are proposals for review. The consumed bonded cycle is not renewed. No value is changed or field released now.

1. **Type-shared equilibrium block:** jointly review four implicated bond r0 families and reproducibly biased junction/tail theta0 families using A/B and full scan geometry. A common CG312-CG312 field covers14-15 and14-16. CF2/CF3 r0 and CG312 triplet theta0 initially remain source-frozen. Do not copy realized QM geometry into equilibrium coefficients or accept a local improvement while the chain worsens.
2. **Identified stiffness only:** after raw Hessian/unit/frame/mode validation and an explicit reduced Jacobian review, admit supported bond/angle force constants. Freeze underdetermined and source-supported coefficients. A high penalty is never evidence of a stiffness defect.
3. **Conditional UB:** initially source-frozen. Require failure of the reduced angle representation on independent curvature plus identifiable UB response. Choose the minimum supported field/group, not four freely varying angle/UB fields. Do not add UB to zero families.
4. **Grouped proper amplitudes:** only once bonded geometry/curvature is satisfactory under the joint objective. Fit the identifiable sum around CC/CS, preserving phase/multiplicity/zero components. Any shared14-16 release requires independent chain targets before adoption. Tail/CF3 amplitudes require their own specific trigger and remain frozen initially.

Every stage retains joint geometry, A/B ordering, global contacts, CC/CS PES, symmetry, branch and compatibility guards. Computational block alternation, if later approved, must still optimize/check one combined potential. Numerical weights, parameter bounds, regularization, rank tolerances, Hessian scaling and prospective 2D/heldout acceptance rules need exact review before any fitting. None is selected from future outcomes.

Prospective heldouts must remain outside fitting, field selection and weight choices. A failed heldout requires a new decision; it does not authorize a second blind cycle. Do not advance if a reduced block remains unidentifiable or only compensating-error fits are possible.
